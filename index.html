About Indian Auto Solutions

Indian Auto Solutions provides trusted tracking technology for personal vehicles, school transport, fleets, and high-value assets. Our platform delivers precision, reliability, and control from one dashboard.

From route planning and emergency alerts to movement history and usage reports, our solutions are designed to improve accountability, reduce risk, and simplify daily operations across urban and intercity transport environments.

Core Features

Each feature is engineered to give control-room clarity with minimal complexity, so teams can monitor movement, prevent misuse, and respond to incidents quickly.

Track Cars, Buses, Bikes and More

Our platform supports seamless tracking for multiple asset categories through one intelligent interface, making it easy for teams to monitor mixed fleets without switching systems.

  • Car: Ideal for personal and company car movement visibility.
  • Bus: Useful for school, staff, and passenger route monitoring.
  • Bike: Effective for quick service teams and field delivery agents.
  • Other assets: Suitable for portable equipment and high-value inventory.

Key Features

Built for real operational workflows, these capabilities help managers and owners reduce uncertainty, coordinate teams better, and maintain an auditable activity history.

  • Share live location: Give controlled access to selected users or supervisors.
  • Multi-asset management: Track all vehicles and assets under a single account.
  • Parking violation alerts: Get immediate notifications on restricted movement zones.
  • Comprehensive reports: Analyze travel, stop, idle and distance in one timeline.
